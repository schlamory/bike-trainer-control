What can this browser actually do?

Runs the full FTMS sequence against the trainer and reports exactly which parts work. The question that matters is whether indications arrive on the control point — without them a naive client looks like the trainer is ignoring it.

This briefly takes control of the trainer and sets a 50 W target, so resistance will change. Don't run it mid-ride.
